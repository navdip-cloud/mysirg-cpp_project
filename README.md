Challenge your word skills with Hangman project.
In this project, user has to guess a word in maximum 6 chances.
You will have to put a letter, if it gets right you will have your lives intact. Keep trying and guessing the word and if you guess it wrong, your chances will get reduced. For every wrong guess, you get your chance reduced by 1.
if you guess the word right in 6 lives, you win the game, otherwise lose.
